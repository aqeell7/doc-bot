# Nimbus Notes — Customer Handbook

Nimbus Notes is a note-taking app for teams. This handbook explains how accounts, plans, billing, sharing, syncing and support work. It is the single source of truth for customer questions.

## 1. Creating an account

You can sign up with an email address or with a Google account. If you sign up with email, we send a confirmation link that stays valid for 24 hours. If the link expires, open the sign-in page and choose "Resend confirmation email". Each email address can only be linked to one Nimbus account. If you already used your work email for a personal account, you must change the personal account's email first before you can use that address for a team account.

Usernames must be between 3 and 20 characters and may contain letters, numbers and underscores. Usernames can be changed once every 30 days from the Profile page. Your old username is released immediately, so another person may take it.

## 2. Plans

Nimbus has three plans: Free, Plus and Team.

The Free plan includes up to 100 notes, 1 GB of attachment storage and sync across two devices. Free notes are kept forever, but attachments larger than 10 MB cannot be uploaded on the Free plan.

The Plus plan costs 6 dollars per month or 60 dollars per year. It removes the note limit, increases storage to 50 GB, allows sync on unlimited devices and unlocks version history for the last 90 days. Plus is meant for one person and cannot be shared with other members.

The Team plan costs 10 dollars per member per month, billed yearly only. It includes everything in Plus, plus shared workspaces, admin controls, single sign-on and version history for the last 365 days. A Team workspace needs at least three members. If a team drops below three members, the workspace keeps working until the end of the current billing year, and the admin is asked to add members or move to Plus at renewal.

## 3. Billing and payments

We accept major credit and debit cards. Bank transfer is available only for Team plans with 20 or more members, and the invoice must be paid within 30 days. Prices do not include local taxes; tax is added at checkout based on your billing address.

Plans renew automatically at the end of each billing period. We email a reminder 7 days before a yearly renewal, but not before a monthly renewal. If a payment fails, we retry the card three times over 10 days. During those 10 days your account keeps working normally. If all three retries fail, the account moves to the Free plan, and any notes above the Free limit become read-only until you upgrade again. Nothing is deleted when this happens.

## 4. Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel at any time from Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your paid features stay active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds depend on the plan and on timing. Monthly Plus payments are not refundable. Yearly Plus payments can be refunded in full if you cancel within 14 days of the payment date. After 14 days, yearly Plus payments are not refunded, but you keep access until the end of the year. Team plans are refunded differently: if a Team admin cancels within 30 days of the yearly payment, we refund the unused months, minus one month as a setup fee. Refunds go back to the original card and usually arrive within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.

If you were charged twice by mistake, contact support with both receipt numbers and we refund the duplicate in full, no matter which plan you are on.

## 5. Notes and attachments

Notes are saved automatically every few seconds while you type. Each note can hold up to 1 million characters. Attachments can be images, PDFs, audio files or any other file type, but executable files are blocked for safety.

Deleted notes go to the Trash and stay there for 30 days. After 30 days they are removed permanently and cannot be recovered, even by support. Plus and Team users can also restore an older version of any note from version history, within the history window of their plan.

## 6. Sharing and permissions

Any note can be shared with a link. Link sharing has three levels: view only, comment and edit. Free users can share notes with view-only links. Plus users can share with all three levels. Team users can additionally share whole folders inside their workspace and set default permissions for new members.

A shared link stops working immediately if the owner turns sharing off or deletes the note. If the owner of a note leaves a Team workspace, their notes are transferred to the workspace admin, and existing share links keep working.

## 7. Syncing and offline use

Nimbus syncs your notes across devices in near real time. If you edit the same note on two devices while one is offline, both versions are kept: the second one to sync is saved as a copy with the word "conflict" in its title, so nothing is lost. You then decide which version to keep.

The desktop and mobile apps work offline. Changes made offline sync automatically the next time the device connects. The web app does not work offline.

## 8. Security and privacy

All notes are encrypted in transit and at rest. Two-factor authentication can be turned on from the Security page and is required for Team admins. We never sell personal data and we never use the content of your notes to train AI models.

If you think someone else has accessed your account, change your password, turn on two-factor authentication and then sign out all other devices from the Security page. Signing out other devices takes effect within one minute.

## 9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Deletion is not instant: the account is first locked for 7 days, so you can change your mind and cancel the deletion by signing in again. After 7 days, all notes, attachments and history are permanently erased. An active paid plan must be cancelled before the account can be deleted, and the cancellation follows the normal refund rules above.

## 10. Getting help

Support is available by email for all plans. Free users get a reply within 3 working days. Plus users get a reply within 1 working day. Team plans include priority support with a reply within 4 working hours, plus a named contact for workspaces with 50 or more members. Support cannot read your notes unless you explicitly grant temporary access from the Help page; that access ends automatically after 48 hours.
